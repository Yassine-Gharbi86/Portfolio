01 — Who I Am

About Me

I'm a 25-year-old Software Engineer currently studying at Holberton School, driven by a deep passion for coding and building things from scratch.

My journey into programming wasn't perfect — in fact, it was full of failed projects, broken code, and long nights trying to figure out why something wasn't working. But every failure taught me something new, and every bug solved pushed me to become a better developer.

What started as curiosity quickly turned into a true passion. I love the process of turning ideas into real, working systems — whether it's designing a backend API, building a mobile app, or creating a clean and interactive user interface.

For me, programming is more than just writing code. It's about solving problems, learning constantly, and pushing myself to build better solutions every day. Every project I work on is another step in my journey to becoming a great software engineer.
